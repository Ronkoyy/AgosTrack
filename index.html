THE GOAL

Protect and Restore Freshwater Ecosystems

Sustainable Development Goal 6 goes beyond drinking water and sanitation to address the quality and sustainability of our global water resources. In communities like ours, rivers, streams, and watersheds are the lifeblood of local agriculture, wildlife, and daily life. Protecting these freshwater sources from pollution and illegal dumping is critical for our future.

Target 6.3: Improve Water Quality

Improve water quality by reducing pollution, eliminating dumping, and minimizing the release of hazardous materials into our rivers and streams.
